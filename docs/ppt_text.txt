Geospatial Disaster Monitoring

PROJECT ABSTRACT:
An adaptive geospatial monitoring framework that intelligently routes heterogeneous satellite observations to specialized expert
models for fast, scalable, and accurate detection and tracking of disaster-related changes across optical, SAR, and thermal data.
PROPOSED SOLUTION:

THE PROBLEM:

Routed Mixture-of-Experts (MoE):
Automatically parses multiconstellation satellite passes, routing
specific orbital bands (SAR for surface
deformation/water, MWIR/TIR for
persistent thermal anomalies) to
specialized expert heads.
Geospatial Foundation Model
Backbones: Leverages frozen PrithviEO and Prithvi-WxC representations,
achieving high temporal and spatial
transferability with low fine-tuning
overhead.

Orbital Data Glut: Earth observation
constellations (Sentinel, INSAT, Landsat)
generate terabytes of imagery every day..
Manual processing creates fatal latency.
Sensor Heterogeneity: Optical, SAR, and
thermal radiometers require isolated
pipelines, resulting in fragmented groundsegment software.
Compute Bottlenecks: Running dense
monolithic models on global 10m-30m
raster tiles overloads ground-station
servers.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY

Technical Feasibility
Built on openly released pre-trained models (Prithvi-EO, PrithviWxC), so no foundation-model pre-training is needed.
Runs on free, open data: Sentinel-1/2, Landsat, MODIS/VIIRS,
CHIRPS, ERA5, plus INSAT (MOSDAC) and Bhuvan for India.
Public benchmarks exist for the experts: Sen1Floods11 (flood),
Landslide4Sense (landslide), D-Fire (fire).
The router activates only the relevant experts per tile, which keeps
ground-station compute low.
Challenges and Mitigation
Cloud cover and night: SAR and thermal bands complement
optical.
Scarce labels: frozen backbones, light fine-tuning, public
benchmarks.
False alarms: per-expert confidence scores, cross-hazard fusion,
and human review before any alert.
Generalization: tested on events, regions and years never seen in
training.

IMPACT AND BENEFITS

Impact:
Disaster Management Agencies: Unified
satellite intelligence for faster response
decisions.
Local Administration: Hazard-specific
maps to identify vulnerable areas and
prioritize evacuation.
Communities: Clear, location-specific
warnings through authorized channels.

Benefits:
Social: Supports timely evacuation and
protection of lives and livestock.
Economic: Helps reduce potential losses
to infrastructure and agriculture.
Environmental: Enables large-area
monitoring of disaster and environmental
changes.
Operational: Hazard-aware routing
enables scalable and efficient processing.

Why our solution is different?
One router replaces separate
per-sensor pipelines, and new
hazards plugin as new experts
without retraining the whole
system (MODULARITY).
